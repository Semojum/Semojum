The desktop application bundles this font unmodified as Assets/Fonts/SimBraille.ttf
and uses it to draw braille cells (Unicode U+2800-U+28FF) and spaces.

The Semojum project owner confirmed on 2026-11-01 that the font may be used and
distributed with the application. No license text was supplied with the font file;
the terms are those of the copyright holder, Duxbury Systems, Inc.
(https://www.duxburysystems.com/).
